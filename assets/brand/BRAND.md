# DIR identity

DIR is pronounced “deer”. The right-path deer combines a calm guide with a
check-shaped antler: the next right action, without pressure or shame.

- Default accent: `#E06A3D` terracotta
- Paper: `#F6F1E8`
- Charcoal: `#1F1F1F`
- Tagline: “The next right thing.”
- Mascot use: onboarding, guidance, empty/error states, and restrained success moments only

`dir-mark.svg` is the deterministic production master. The generated board is
art direction; production assets must continue to derive from the SVG geometry.
