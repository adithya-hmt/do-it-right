# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Be kind, assume good intent, give specific feedback, and respect different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Reporting

Report a concern privately to the repository maintainers. Please do not open a public issue containing sensitive details.
